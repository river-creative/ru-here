Read these before the week starts — attendance, serving, seating and dress code all change during conference.

Reminders

Attendance
Attending MLC is mandatory. Sign in will be open by 8:00 AM & 6:00 PM at the Registration Tent. Do not leave the AM service until the end unless you have a job and please leave discreetly. (No SW Lab or MSP during MLC).
Guest speakers
Monday-Friday you need to be seated by 8:30 AM in Studio B if you are not serving. No one should be in the lobby at this time.
Saturday
Even though we do not have a guest speaker Saturday AM, you should not skip out on Saturdays. Your attendance is mandatory on Saturday unless you are working.
Helps
1st Years August students are to serve 1 Sunday and 2 morning services. 2nd and 3rd year students will be scheduled for 4 services during the week in addition to your assigned Sunday. Your assigned MOH is the priority.
Choir
Choir is not included in your required 4 services of MOH. Please do your very best to coordinate your MOH and your choir responsibilities by communicating with your managers. They are expecting your communication.
Seating
Please see the special seaters before you take or reserve a seat. Guests and ministers on the frontline come to receive and we ask that you please give them preferred seating. Please do not save seats as your things will be collected and put in “Lost and Found”.
Student services
RU offices will be closed during MLC. Therefore, there will be no student services.
Parking
Please make sure that you are parking in designated student parking. Additionally, please remain adaptable, as there may be instances when parking in the gravel lot is necessary. Remember to be respectful of the Parking Team.
Dresscode
Student class dress code is required for the week, we will be monitoring. All students in casual dress will be sent home to change.

Tips

Prepare in the natural to receive from the Supernatural!

  • Do all laundry for conference week this weekend
  • Prepare meals in advance
  • Nap in between services
  • Eat a good breakfast
  • Come hungry, thirsty and ready to receive everything the Lord has for you!
